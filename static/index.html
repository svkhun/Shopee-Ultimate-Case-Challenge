Mathematical Derivation: How 85% of Failed Deliveries is Calculated
Pure Analytical Calculation

The 85% metric is calculated directly from the three given real numbers without needing simulated data:

Step 1: COD Failures
35% × 2.61% = 0.9135%
COD failure contribution per total platform orders.
Step 2: Non-COD Failures
65% × 0.25% = 0.1625%
Non-COD failure contribution per total platform orders.
Step 3: Total Failures
0.9135% + 0.1625% = 1.076%
Total platform delivery failure rate across all payments.
Step 4: COD Concentration
0.9135% ÷ 1.076% ≈ 84.9% ≈ 85%
85% of all failed deliveries originate strictly from COD orders!
Three Core Drivers of COD Delivery Failure

Behavioral and logistical factors explaining why COD fails at 10.6× higher rate

1. Zero Financial Commitment

Buyers bear no upfront financial cost at checkout. Refusing parcels incurs no monetary loss, driving casual impulse orders that are easily rejected at the doorstep.

2. Unsynchronized Delivery Timing

Couriers arrive unannounced when buyers are not home or lack cash on hand. Without scheduled delivery windows, first-attempt failure rates soar.

3. Disproportionate Platform Strain

Because 85% of all delivery failures stem from COD orders, targeting COD unreliability solves the overwhelming majority of failed parcel waste on Shopee.

Benefits For Buyers

Frictionless convenience and recipient empowerment

  • Greater delivery convenience — Recipient chooses when they are present and ready to pay.
  • Fewer missed deliveries — Eliminates courier arrival when buyer is at work or running errands.
  • More control over delivery timing — Empowers buyers with predictable, scheduled parcel handover.
Benefits For Shopee & Sellers

Operational efficiency and lower logistics cost

  • Higher first-attempt delivery success — Parcels successfully received without multiple courier trips.
  • Lower re-delivery and return costs — Substantially cuts reverse logistics handling waste.
  • Increased customer satisfaction — Improves NPS and buyer retention across both COD and prepaid cohorts.
