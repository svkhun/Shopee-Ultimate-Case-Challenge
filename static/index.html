Key Insights & Behavioral Mechanics

Why Cash on Delivery fails at 10.6× higher frequency

01
Zero Financial Commitment at Checkout

Buyers bear no immediate financial loss if they refuse delivery, fostering casual impulse orders that are later abandoned when rider arrives.

02
Unsynchronized Delivery Timing

Without scheduled delivery windows, couriers arrive unannounced when buyers are away, leading to multiple failed attempts and ultimate RTO.

03
Disproportionate Reverse Logistics Waste

At THB 45 per return shipment, COD failures drain merchants and platform unit economics while tying up inventory for 5–7 days.
